An AI trading terminal closes that gap: it ingests live market data, runs it through analytical models, and returns an interpretation — key levels, setup quality, risk context — in plain English. You still make the decision. The terminal compresses the hours of analysis that decision used to require into seconds.</p> <p>Tradolux does this with five specialized agents that run simultaneously on every ticker you analyze, using a live IEX data feed with sub-second updates.</p> <h2>The five agents, and what each one reads</h2> <ul> <li><strong><a href="/agent-chart">Chart Intelligence</a></strong> — price structure, support and resistance, VWAP, moving averages, volume. Answers: what is the tape actually doing?</li> <li><strong><a href="/agent-sentiment">Sentiment</a></strong> — social volume, news flow, options skew. Answers: what is the crowd positioned for?</li> <li><strong><a href="/agent-fundamental">Fundamental</a></strong> — revenue growth, margins, valuation versus sector. Answers: does the business justify the price?</li> <li><strong><a href="/agent-risk">Risk Management</a></strong> — ATR, volatility regime, stop distance, position sizing. Answers: how much can this trade hurt you?</li> <li><strong><a href="/agent-macro">Macro</a></strong> — rates, sector rotation, index context. Answers: is the market tide with you or against you?</li> </ul> <p>Each agent returns its own verdict — bullish, neutral, caution, headwind — with a one-line justification. You see where they agree and, just as importantly, where they conflict.</p> <h2>From five reads to one probabilistic assessment</h2> <p>The agents' outputs are combined into a single assessment with a confidence score. When conditions align, the terminal generates a Smart Signal from nine detectors — power inflow, VWAP reclaim, opening-range breakout, failed breakdown, RSI divergence, and others — each with a defined entry, target, and stop. A signal is a structured hypothesis with its risk pre-attached, not a prediction.</p> <p>Every observation is logged, resolved, and scored over time, so you can see how each agent and detector has actually performed — not just how it claims to perform.</p> <h2>What it deliberately does not do</h2> <p>No AI system predicts the future. Tradolux does not promise win rates, does not auto-trade your account, and is not financial advice. It is an analysis layer: it makes the state of the market legible fast, states probabilities and risk explicitly, and leaves the decision with you. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.</p> <h2>How traders fit it into a real routine</h2> <p>Swing traders run their watchlist through the terminal after the close: five reads per ticker, conflicts flagged, levels marked — a full end-of-day review in minutes. End-of-day options sellers use the Risk and Macro agents to sanity-check volatility regime and stop distance before selling premium into the next session. Intraday, <a href="/features">smart alerts</a> track your levels so you don't have to watch the screen.</p> <h2>See it work on a live ticker</h2> <p>The fastest way to understand the terminal is to watch it read a real chart.
